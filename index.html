How this estimate works

Transparent assumptions

The calculator uses relative GPU and CPU performance tiers, resolution and graphics-load multipliers, plus a memory-pressure adjustment. Because there are no verified GTA VI PC tests, the output is deliberately shown as a broad estimate range with low confidence. It is not sourced GTA VI benchmark data.

Laptop graphics cards are listed separately from desktop models because power limits and cooling can make similarly named GPUs perform very differently.
